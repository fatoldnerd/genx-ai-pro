## Discovery notes

Maya said: "Every proposal starts from a blank page even though most of the evidence already exists in call notes, old scopes, and consultant emails. It is inconsistent and I still rewrite almost everything before it goes out."

Maya said: "Client status reporting is the other problem. The team sends updates in different formats, risks are easy to bury, and I cannot tell quickly which client needs attention."

Current proposal process:

1. A consultant finishes a discovery call.
2. Notes are saved in a mixture of Google Docs and email.
3. Alex finds a previous proposal that looks similar.
4. A new draft is assembled manually.
5. Maya reviews all scope, pricing, claims, and external wording.
6. The proposal is sent manually after approval.

Current client-reporting process:

1. Consultants submit weekly notes in different formats.
2. Alex consolidates the updates manually.
3. Maya reviews client risks and commitments.
4. The final report is sent manually.

Maya estimates that a normal proposal draft takes between six and eight working hours before her review. This is synthetic input for workflow testing, not a measured GenX AI Pro result or a promised saving.

## Known pain

- Proposal creation is repeated and inconsistent.
- Relevant evidence exists but is scattered.
- Founder review is required before any commercial commitment.
- Client-status notes are inconsistent and obscure risk.
- The business does not want a custom software project or production integration in the first phase.

## Desired business outcome

- Produce more consistent first drafts from approved source material.
- Make assumptions, exclusions, missing evidence, and review points visible.
- Standardise weekly client-status summaries without sending them automatically.
- Reduce blank-page work while keeping commercial and client decisions under human control.

## Acceptance criteria

### Proposal workflow

- Every factual claim traces to supplied material or is labelled as an assumption.
- Pricing, legal terms, delivery commitments, and external sending remain under Maya's approval.
- Scope, assumptions, exclusions, missing evidence, risks, and next steps are explicit.
- The draft must not invent ROI, case studies, integrations, deadlines, or buyer intent.

### Client-status workflow

- Every client fact traces to supplied consultant notes.
- Risks, owners, blockers, and next actions are visible.
- Missing information is identified rather than inferred.
- No report is sent automatically.
