## 2. Executive proposal summary

**Confirmed:** Northstar Cyber Advisory currently assembles proposal drafts manually from discovery notes, old scopes, Google Docs, and consultant emails. Maya Singh then reviews all scope, pricing, claims, and external wording before manual sending. Weekly client updates also arrive in inconsistent formats, requiring Alex Romero to consolidate them before Maya reviews risks and commitments.

The proposed **GenX AI Pro Private AI Agent Buildout Sprint  -  Starter package** is a £5,000, two-week engagement covering two workflows:

1. Discovery notes and approved historical material to a reviewed proposal and SOW first draft.
2. Consultant updates to a reviewed client-status summary.

The engagement is intended to reduce blank-page assembly, improve consistency, and make source gaps, assumptions, exclusions, risks, and review points visible. Pricing, legal terms, delivery commitments, claims, client decisions, and external sending remain under Maya’s approval. The Sprint does not include integrations, automatic sending, legal approval, certification, custom application development, or more than two workflows.
